# Touchpoint Workspace (`src/app/page.tsx`)

- **Scope & Mode**: Full web application shell, Dashboard, Contacts Directory, Circles & Groups, Relationships Map, Calendar & Schedule, Settings, Help & Guide, and all Modals (`Operate` mode with `Help & Guide` in `Read` mode).
- **Audience & Task**: Self-hosted CRM operators triaging relationship cadences, filtering/bulk-editing contacts, and logging touchpoints without losing a single existing capability.
- **Constraints**: 100% zero feature loss across all 7 tabs, all 6 Settings accordion sections, and all modals. Both Light and Dark modes supported within the SBB Chronometer Dispatch identity.
